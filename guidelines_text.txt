1. Objective of PRE-IDEATHON
➢ PRE-IDEATHON 2026 is designed to provide students with a platform to identify genuine real-world
problems, develop innovative solutions, evaluate their feasibility, and present them as potential ventures.
➢ The competition will focus not only on how innovative an idea is, but also on whether the idea can
realistically be implemented, converted into a sustainable business, generate revenue, and create measurable
impact.
➢ Problem → Solution → Validation → Implementation → Market → Revenue → Growth
3. Idea/Problem Statement
➢ Teams should clearly explain:
What is the problem?
Who faces the problem?
How serious/frequent is it?
What existing solutions are available?
Why are existing solutions insufficient?
➢ Ideas that merely identify a problem without proposing a meaningful solution may receive lower evaluation
scores.
4. Originality & Intellectual Property
➢ The proposed idea must be the team's own work.
➢ Plagiarism, copying, or unauthorized use of another team's idea will not be permitted.
➢ Existing technologies or business models may be used as inspiration, but teams must demonstrate
meaningful innovation, differentiation, or improvement.
➢ If an existing product/service is being improved, the team must clearly explain:
What already exists?
What is being changed?
Why is the proposed version better?

➢ The organizers will not claim ownership of participants' ideas unless separately agreed through an official
written process.
5. Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)
➢ AI tools may be used for assistance in areas such as:
➢ Brainstorming, Research assistance, Language improvement, Presentation design, Data organization,
Preliminary analysis.
➢ However the core idea, reasoning, validation, business logic, and pitch must represent the team's own
understanding and work.
➢ Teams must be able to explain every major aspect of their proposal during Q&A.
➢ Using AI to fabricate research, customer data, financial figures, testimonials, or other evidence is prohibited.
6. Event Format & Time Limit
➢ Each team will receive approximately:
4-5 minutes – Idea Presentation
3-5 minutes – Judges' Q&A
➢ Teams must complete their pitch within the allotted time.
➢ No additional time will normally be provided unless an official technical issue affecting the event.
7. Presentation Requirements
➢ Each team should prepare a 6–8 slide PPT presentation.
➢ The presentation should preferably cover: Problem statement, Target users, Proposed solution, How it
works, Key features, Innovation & Differentiation, USP, Existing alternatives, Implementation & Business
Model roadmap, Required resources/technology, Target market, Revenue model, unit economics, Impact &
Future Growth strategy.
➢ Teams may modify the structure according to their idea, provided that the essential aspects are covered.
➢ Since PRE-IDEATHON focuses on ideas with potential to become real ventures, teams should demonstrate
a realistic implementation pathway.
➢ A strong idea without a realistic implementation pathway may receive a lower score under the
implementation criterion.
8. Business & Revenue Potential
➢ Business potential will be a major component of evaluation.
➢ Teams should demonstrate how their solution can potentially become financially sustainable.
➢ Teams should explain:
Who will pay for the solution?
Is the market local, regional, national, or global?
Revenue Model
➢ Examples may include: Direct product sales, Subscription, Commission, Licensing, Freemium, Service
fees, B2B contracts, Marketplace model, Advertising, Pay-per-use, Pricing
➢ Teams should have a basic understanding of:
➢ Production/development, Customer acquisition, Distribution
➢ Maintenance and Other major costs Profitability.
➢ Important: Exact financial projections are not compulsory at the PRE-IDEATHON stage. However,
assumptions should be logical, realistic, and defensible.
